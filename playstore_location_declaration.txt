GOOGLE PLAY LOCATION DECLARATION

APP PURPOSE

Totals is a personal finance app that helps users track income, expenses, balances, budgets, and spending. With permission, it reads supported bank transaction SMS messages and processes them locally on the device into a private transaction history. Users can also add, split, and categorize transactions manually.

LOCATION ACCESS

Spending Map collects precise location to attach a place to new debit and credit transactions, including supported bank SMS transactions received while Totals is closed or not in use. Background access is needed because these messages can arrive at any time; foreground-only access would miss them. Coordinates are stored locally, are not used for advertising or analytics, and can be deleted by the user at any time.
